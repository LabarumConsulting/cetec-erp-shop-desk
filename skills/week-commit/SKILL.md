# Week commit

Use the shop's own Cetec MCP. One week, not the whole backlog. Do not invent dates. Do not move a work order.

## When to use

- "Will we hit the dock this week?"
- Acknowledgments due in five days that are short or stuck.

## Steps

1. List orders or work orders due in the next five working days.
2. Keep the ones short on a component or sitting in one location past three working days.
3. Name the customer, the promise date, and the block.
4. Do not reschedule anything.

## Return

Order, customer, due date, and short or stuck. If the week is clean, say so.
