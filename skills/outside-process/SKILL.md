# Outside process

Use the shop's own Cetec MCP. Do not invent vendors or jobs. Do not receive a PO.

## When to use

- "What is still out at the plater?"
- Jobs that left for outside process and have not come back.

## Steps

1. List open POs that are outside process, not material. If the tool cannot tell a vendor process PO from a material PO, say so and stop.
2. Keep the ones past due or holding a work order.
3. Name the vendor, the part, and the job if known.
4. Do not receive anything.

## Return

Vendor, process if known, part, job, days out. If the distinction is missing, stop.
